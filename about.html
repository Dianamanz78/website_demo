A Little About Me

Hi, I’m Hanani! I’m a computer science student at Pittsburgh who enjoys trying new things and finding a good balance between work and life. I like having a project to work on, a place to explore, or something new to learn, but I also appreciate the moments of quieter life that make every day more meaningful. I’m always drawn to a mix of spending time with other people, expressing creativity, and solving problems. Working toward a solution brings me as much satisfaction as the outcome. I think life is more satisfying when I’m open-minded about new experiences-- professional or otherwise.

What I’m Working Toward

Right now, I’m focused on building a career that allows me to continue learning, take on challenges, and contribute to the public. My interest in computer science satisfies my desire to solve complex problems and work in teams. Building my playlist voting app has given me experience in tackling a real-world problem by using my knowledge of software. This collaborative experience prepared me to work with a professional team.

How I Approach Life

I often describe myself as curious, dependable, and willing to learn. Learning how things work is a must, as is understanding the people I’m working with. I want to know what motivates others so I can work well with them. Being involved in team sports has taught me patience often matters more than talent. I respect others by being punctual and by being willing to admit when I don’t understand something. I don’t mind being the first to admit a mistake because it allows me to be relatable to those I work with.
